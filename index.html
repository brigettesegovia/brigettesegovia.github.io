About

I am interested in galaxy formation, dark matter halos, stellar streams, satellite disruption, orbital dynamics, and computational astrophysics. This site collects my research, projects, visualizations, and academic work.

Research

My work focuses on understanding how galaxies evolve within dark matter halos, how satellite galaxies lose mass through tidal stripping, and how simulations can connect theoretical models to observable structure.

Dark Matter Halos

Density profiles, substructure, halo mass functions, and dynamical evolution.

Satellite Disruption

Simulations of tidal stripping, bound mass evolution, and orbital decay.

Stellar Streams

Stream formation, perturbations, particle-spray methods, and visualization.
